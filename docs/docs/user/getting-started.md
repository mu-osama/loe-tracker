# User Guide

## What this application does

PixelEDGE helps you record daily effort, review your monthly progress, and submit one monthly sheet for approval.

## Demo login credentials

- **Admin:** `admin@company.com` / `Admin@1234`
- **User:** `user@company.com` / `User@1234`

Use the admin account for setup, oversight, and review. Use the user account for LOE entry and submission.

## First steps

1. Sign in with your company account.
2. Open **Dashboard** to see the current month summary.
3. Open **My LOE** and choose the target month.
4. Enter hours daily against assigned projects and shared categories.
5. Save draft progress as you work.
6. Submit the monthly sheet when the month is complete.

## Important concepts

- **Assigned projects** are the projects currently allocated to you.
- **Shared fixed categories** are organization-wide categories such as non-project time buckets.
- **Draft** means your month is still editable.
- **Submitted** means the sheet is locked until reopened by an authorized reviewer.
- **Approved** means the month is closed.
- **Reopened** means the month was sent back for correction and is editable again.

## What to expect in the current demo environment

The current seed database is intentionally clean. It includes one admin, one user, sample projects, shared fixed categories, and active allocations for the seeded user. It does not preload old submitted or approved month history.

For the full end-to-end path, continue to [User Flow](./flow.md).
